Three small mechanics that stopped my nagging

2026-05-02

"Hurry up." "Did you pack your stuff?" "Have you done your homework?" How many times a day do you say these? I stopped counting. Too many.

But over the past six months, I almost never say them anymore. Three small mechanics did it:

1. Make the tasks visible
A wall chart, a checklist app, anything. If the kid can check "what's next" on their own, you don't have to.

2. Create a small "done" moment
A sticker, an animation. If finishing feels good, they'll keep going.

3. Decide not to scold mistakes
Forgot something? Stay quiet. One uncomfortable consequence teaches more than ten reminders.

Building a system the kid wants to use is far easier than dragging them through one.
