A tiny tweak that fixed our slow mornings

2026-05-05

My son was terrible at mornings. No matter how many times I reminded him, he wouldn't move. "Brush your teeth." "Get dressed." "Eat your breakfast." Every word from me. We were both miserable.

The fix was tiny: I put each task on a card and let him pick the order, one card at a time.

"Which one next?" He'd think for a second, grab one, and do it. No complaints — because he chose the order.

This is exactly what became the "Gacha" app. The same task feels completely different when the kid chose it themselves.

Adults are the same. Same task, but if you feel forced into it, it's painful. Mornings are no different for kids.
