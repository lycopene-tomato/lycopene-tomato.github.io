What I do

Apps: every app on the Lyco App list — TodoGacha, TodoBingo, TodoSugoroku, TodoFarm, TodoTimer, TodoPuzzle, TroubleNote — is something I tried with my own kid first. The theme is "how kids' motivation actually moves", explored from slightly different angles.

Column: on the Blog I write about what's on my mind while building, and what I tried at home. Sometimes I borrow engineering frames (TDD, BDD) for parenting; sometimes I read ADHD research to inform app design.

Where I stand

★ Transparency about expertise

I am not a medical, clinical, or educational professional. I read ADHD research and cognitive science papers on my own, but everything on this site is a personal note from an indie dev and parent, not medical advice or diagnosis. If something concerns you, please consult a qualified professional.

Site principles

  • No labeling kids — I don't write "ADHD kids" or "kids with developmental disorders" as a category
  • No comparison shaming — no "neurotypical kids" or "different from normal kids" framing
  • No medical claims or diagnosis — no "this might be ADHD" speculation
  • Citations matter — when I draw from ADHD research, I link the paper or book
  • Kids' privacy is protected — no photos, no real names; situations are altered
  • I don't blame parents — no "it's the parent's fault" or "yelling doesn't help" framing
