Earning Trust — Empathy and Honesty That Survive the Fact-Check

2026.07.11~4 min

00要約Overview

The middle of DECAX is Engage and Check. Being discovered is not being used — a stranger's app doesn't get installed on sight, and an individual has no brand standing behind them. So the articles do the work: empathy first, then an honesty that survives verification. No embellishment; only what helps. Trust is built from empathy and honesty, and from nothing else.

01物語Story

Situation

3.3.1 Getting discovered brought people in through search. DECAX moves next to Engage (a relationship with the writer) and Check (verifying the content).

Complication

Discovery is not use. People don't just install an app made by a stranger. No name recognition, no corporate brand at your back. "Can this person be trusted? Is this information real?" — that's what a visitor checks first.

Question

With no brand behind you — how do you earn trust?

02解決Solution

Criteria

  • Trust earned without brand or ad spend
  • Withstands a reader's verification
  • Sustainable — a way of writing that doesn't strain

Answer

Through the articles, build empathy (Engage) and verification-proof honesty (Check). Concretely, three rules held: don't embellish. Write only what helps. Don't hide your own failures. A page of nothing but upsides reads as an advertisement in seconds — while honestly written struggle and failure earn the empathy of people carrying the same problem, and that empathy becomes trust in the writer.

Reason

Because trust is built from empathy and honesty, and from nothing else. An individual has no brand to shortcut with. Exaggeration collapses the moment a reader checks it — and collapsed trust does not come back. Honest writing that survives the check converts, slowly, into trust.

With no shortcut available, only the honest road remains.
Which turns out to be the strongest one anyway.

Options

  • Inflate the numbers for attention — eye-catching, briefly. It cannot survive Check, and when it falls it takes the trust down with it. Not taken.

03結果Result

Good

Empathy formed, and with it the thought: "if it's this person's work, I'll give it a try." Trust that can't be bought with ads — the kind that survives being checked — accrues, a little at a time.

Bad

Trust takes ages to build and one moment to break. A single dishonest article can spend all of it. The commitment is to stay honest — permanently.

Follow-up

The people who trusted you and tried the product can become the ones who carry it outward. That's 3.3.3 Turning users into fans.
