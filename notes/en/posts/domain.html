Getting a Custom Domain — the Asset at the Center of Your Brand

2026.07.12~3 min

00要約Overview

A custom domain is not just about the URL. It is the address of your website, the base of your custom email, the proof behind your AdMob ad verification and your search-engine ownership claims. Own one domain, and brand machinery stacks on top of it, piece after piece. Of everything in the brand foundation, it is the most broadly applicable asset — which is why it sits at the center.

01動機Why

If a custom domain is just "a way to make the URL look sharp," that's a waste of it. A custom domain is the central asset of the whole brand.

Register it once, and the website, the custom email address, the ad verification, the search-engine registration — all of it rides on the same domain. And when the day comes to move the service elsewhere, you move by repointing the domain. A domain is the brand's address, and its ground.

02方法How

The work: register one custom domain, then reuse it as the brand's foundation. On that ground, stack the pieces one at a time — publishing the website, the custom email, the ad verification, the search registration. Each has its own article below this one.

03価値What

Own one domain, and the machinery that rides on it multiplies on its own. URL, email, ads, search — things that would each be a chore to arrange separately all derive from a single asset.

A custom domain is not a URL.
It is the ground every piece of your brand stands on.
