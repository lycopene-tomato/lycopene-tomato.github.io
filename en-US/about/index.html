Where this site stands

★ Where this site stands

This site is one engineer-who-parents sharing solo app dev work — it's not a medical info site. I'm not a parenting expert, not a medical expert — just an engineer in the middle of parenting.

What I write is my own parenting log, not about other kids. When I read ADHD papers, it's to inform my app design — not medical advice.

References here are notes for app features, not claims of medical authority. If something concerns you, please consult a qualified professional.

How I write here

  • No labeling, no comparison shaming — I don't write "ADHD kids" or "different from neurotypical kids" framing
  • No medical claims or diagnosis — no "this might be ADHD" speculation
  • Citations matter — when I borrow from research, I link the paper or book
  • Kids' privacy is protected — no photos, no real names; situations are altered
  • I don't blame parents — no "it's the parent's fault" or "yelling doesn't help" framing (I'm one of those parents too)

I'm figuring this out day by day too. From one parent to another, what I write is just what I tried.

FAQ

Is this site medical advice?

No. This is personal experience — not medical advice or diagnostic claims. When I cite research, I'm reading it as input for app design.

Why do you cite ADHD research?

As an engineer, understanding "how people actually start moving" and "how memory works" helps me build better tools. The citations are app design inputs, not medical recommendations.

Are the apps free or paid?

All apps are free. This site is also fully free to read.

What languages do you support?

The site is in Japanese / English (en-US) / Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant) / Korean (ko). App Store listings cover 50+ locales.

Can I link to this site freely?

Yes — this site is link-free. You're welcome to share URLs to any page or post as text links without asking. If it helps even one more parent breathe a little easier, that's the whole point.
