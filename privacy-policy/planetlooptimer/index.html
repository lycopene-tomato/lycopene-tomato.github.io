Privacy Policy

PlanetLoopTimer — by Lyco App

The short version: PlanetLoopTimer is a focus and break timer for general use. We do not collect any personal information, do not request the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not use analytics SDKs, and do not share data with third parties. All your timer settings stay on your device.

Scope

This privacy policy applies to PlanetLoopTimer, an iOS app developed by Lyco App. The app is a focus / break timer intended for general use (including parents and other adults managing their time). This page, linked from the app's App Store listing, serves as the privacy policy for the app.

Information We Collect

  • Timer settings and usage state are stored only on your device.
  • We do not transmit your data to our servers or any external service.
  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
  • We do not request or collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Advertising

The current version of the app shows no advertisements. If advertising is added in a future update, the policy will be updated in advance and the advertising identifier will still not be requested unless this section explicitly states otherwise.

Analytics & Tracking

The app uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. No usage data is sent off the device. Apple's built-in anonymized crash and performance reports (which require the user to opt in via system settings) may be received from Apple, with no personally identifiable information attached.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user information with third parties.

Children

The app is not specifically designed for or marketed to children. Regardless of the user's age, the app collects no personal information, no account, no advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics. The protections above apply uniformly to every user.
